Pairing backup

Your NotePin only connects with two keys that Plaud's cloud issues for it. Plaud V2 also keeps a copy in your login Keychain, so the pairing survives a cleaner app deleting Plaud V2's data. You can also export them to a file, for another Mac or as an extra backup.

Connect your NotePin to export its pairing.

Unpairs your NotePin so it can pair with another phone or computer. To use it here again, scan and pair it again.
